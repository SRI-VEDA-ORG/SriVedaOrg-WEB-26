The Project Includes A Complete Hospital Website With Dedicated Pages Such As Home, About Hospital, Doctors, Specialties, Facilities, Services, OPD Timings, Gallery, Contact, And Online OP Registration. Patients Can Conveniently Access Online OP Registration Through The Website Link And QR Code From Home. The Website Is Designed To Provide Clear Hospital Information In A Responsive, User-Friendly, And Professional Format.
The OP Management System Handles Patient Registration, OP Details, OP Print, And Payment Receipts, While Doctor Login Provides Authorized Access To Patient OP And Payment Information, Including Previous-Date Records. The Pharmacy Management System Includes Medicine Billing, Inventory, Medicine Registration, And Medicine Reports, With Billing And Medicine Details Securely Stored In The Database For Quick Retrieval.
The Staff Attendance System Is Integrated With The ZK Teco MB30 Device And Displays Attendance Records Using Indian Standard Time (IST). Public Hospital Information Is Available Through The Website, While Patient Details, OP Records, Payment Information, And Hospital Forms Are Protected Through Authorized Access.
